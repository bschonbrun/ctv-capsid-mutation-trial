NOTES FOR Q&A
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Why not test p33 K174R instead of p65+p61?
A: Literature support and synergy. Shilts 2020 shows p33 alone = 16.3 pp gain.
   But p33 mechanism is unknown (could be titer/fitness). p65+p61 is binding
   (known mechanism). Start with the clearer target; add p33 if needed.

Q: What if individual genes do work in our hands (contradiction to Harper)?
A: Possible, but requires >300 aphids per gene to have power to detect 5% effect.
   We recommend both-together first (matches Harper), then individual as Phase 2.

Q: How do we know these mutations won't break assembly?
A: We test in N. benthamiana first (agroinfiltration, RT-qPCR). If replicates
   normally, assembly is likely fine (HSPs assist but don't form virion core).

Q: Can we speed this up by testing in citrus from day 1?
A: No. N. benthamiana validation prevents wasting citrus plants. Also, citrus
   validation takes 4-6 months (greenhouse); N. bent. validation is 2-3 weeks.

Q: Why FS577 background instead of T68-1 (44.2% transmission)?
A: Good question. FS577 is 24.1% (same phenotype as T36 genotype). Clearer
   target for "disable transmission." Could test T68-1 as Phase 2 reciprocal.
