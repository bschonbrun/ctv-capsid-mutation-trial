                    REVISED PICTURE: THREE STUDIES, THREE GENES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HARPER 2016 (University of Florida lab)
  Genes tested:     p65 + p61 (HSP70 and HSP90 homologs)
  Construct:        T36 infectious clone with FS577 substitutions
  Result:           p65+p61 together → 17.9% (from 0.6%)
                    Each alone → 1.9% and 4.0% respectively
  Mechanism:        NOT titer-based (RT-qPCR shows no difference)
                    "Concerted action" suggests cooperativity
  Sample size:      n=196 for p65+p61 hybrid, n=253 for full p6-p18 region
  Conclusion:       p65 and p61 sequences are critical for transmission


SHILTS 2020 (same lab + others)
  Genes tested:     p33 + p18 + p13 (triple deletion & restoration)
  Construct:        T36 with all three genes deleted, then restored from FS577
  Result:           p33+p18+p13 together → ~50% (from ~0.6%)
  Mechanism:        Unknown (titer not reported)
  Sample size:      Not visible in abstract (paper needs retrieval)
  Conclusion:       These three genes matter, but can't separate which drives effect


SHILTS 2026 (same lab + others, most recent)
  Genes tested:     p33 + p18 + p13 (triple deletion, same as Shilts 2020)
  Construct:        Same as Shilts 2020 but now with sequence analysis
  Result:           Triple substitution → 50% (from 0.6%)
                    Sequence analysis: "only p33 differs" with K174R change
  Mechanism:        Unknown (titer not reported)
  Sample size:      Not visible in abstract
  Conclusion:       p33 is the key driver, but tested as part of triple substitution
