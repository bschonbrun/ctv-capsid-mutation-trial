# Round 2 — Adversarial Rebuttal — claude-opus-5

*This reviewer is **Reviewer G** in the anonymised dossier. It was shown all five round-1 rebuttals from the other models and asked to concede, withdraw, rebut, and re-rank. This was written after reading all five rebuttal arguments, before composing this response.*

---

## 1. CONCEDED — where another reviewer beat me

**Reviewer A: the distinction between "permissive" and "necessary" was implicit in my round-1 critique but A states it more cleanly.** I said p33 is "necessary and large but partial," which gestured at permissiveness without naming it. A's framework—that a background-specific substitution effect justifies exploratory design without establishing necessity or sufficiency—is the right one. I should have led with that decision-theoretic move instead of treating exploratory planning and evidence for a "transmission determinant" as the same claim.

**Reviewer B: the shared-K174 argument is airtight.** I noted the T36 clone contains K174, making the gain independent of a K↔R swap. B identified that **both recipient and donor encode K174**, so any observed gain from the full p33 region cannot be attributed to the substituted residue. This is stronger than my formulation and closes the loop on why K174R as a construct target is indefensible.

**Reviewer D: unmeasured source-plant titer is the most dangerous confound I understated.** p33 is a viroporin; I flagged this but treated it as one among several confounds. D and E elevate it correctly: a p33 swap that increases virion yield or phloem loading looks like a transmission determinant in this assay but is not one. Without titre normalisation, the entire inference chain could be tilted by systemic fitness rather than vector specificity. This is CHECKABLE and absent.

## 2. WITHDRAWN — my own claims that do not survive

> "The reciprocal swap: T68-1 carrying T36's p33, at n ≥ 200 aphids... is the only experiment that can *break* the hypothesis."

I overstated the uniqueness. It is the single most informative missing experiment, but B, D, E and F correctly identify that pulling Harper 2016 and Shilts 2026 full texts is equally load-bearing. My framing made the reciprocal sound like a prerequisite; it is best-positioned *among* the prerequisites. I withdraw "only" and "uniquely."

> "The brief updated where reviewers found real gaps — with the changes marked, so your team can see what review changed."

I projected this as something the authors should do. That is outside the scope of scientific review. I should have named the gaps (which all six now agree on) and stopped there.

## 4. SURVIVING FINDINGS — ranked by consensus and checkability

### Unanimous across all six reviewers:

1. **[CHECKABLE] K174R is incompatible with the brief's sequences.** T36 clone EU937521 encodes K174 yet transmits at 1.5%; T68-1 also K174 yet transmits at 44.2%; the p33 swap (moving T68-1 p33 into T36 clone, both K174) gains 16.3 percentage points, not attributable to a K↔R change. T30 and FS577 both R174 yet differ ~15-fold. The construct targeting K174R has no basis.

2. **[CHECKABLE] FS577 "24.1%" lacks a denominator and cannot anchor quantitative claims.** It is cited to establish a transmission-CPm link and to ground the "16-fold gap" arithmetic, but without N it is unverifiable. This weakens two inference chains simultaneously.

3. **[CHECKABLE + JUDGEMENT] The p61/p65 "residual" of 5.2 percentage points is not a verified gap.** Harper 2016's 23% (n unknown, unread) minus Shilts 2020's 17.8% (n=90, CI 10.5–27.3%) is a cross-study subtraction with overlapping confidence intervals and unknown vector/assay equivalence. Power to detect a genuine 5.2-point difference at n=90/arm is ~14%; ~950/arm would be needed for 80% power. No statistical basis exists for attributing a gap to p61/p65.

4. **[JUDGEMENT] The p33 effect is real and statistically significant (16/90 vs 1/66, p ≈0.0012) but does not establish a transmission *determinant*.** The effect size is large; the mechanism is unidentified (could be virion yield, phloem loading, or vector binding — untested). This is enough to justify further investigation, not enough to justify a multi-construct panel targeting that mechanism.

5. **[CHECKABLE] Source-plant viral titer was not measured, so p33's role in virion accumulation vs. vector interaction cannot be separated.** p33 is a viroporin. A swap that increases titer looks like a transmission determinant in an assay that does not normalize titer. This confound is verifiable and absent from the evidence.

6. **[JUDGEMENT] No reciprocal swap (T36 p33 → T68-1) has been performed.** Every experiment shown moves T68-1 sequence into T36. Without testing whether T36's p33 impairs transmission in the high-transmitter background, the hypothesis remains permissive rather than causal.

7. **[CHECKABLE] The 16-fold baseline gap is arithmetic conflation.** T68-1 (95/215) vs T36 (1/66) = ~29.2-fold. FS577 (24.1%) vs T36 (1.5%) = 16-fold. The brief's framing ("16-fold difference between high (T68-1) and low (T36)") conflates them. A and B both caught this.

8. **[CHECKABLE] Harper 2016 and Shilts 2026 remain unread and secondary.** The brief cites a paywalled Harper paper without denominators and a 2026 abstract without methods. A "coordinated function" statement from an abstract is not methods-level evidence. These must be retrieved before designing constructs that target their putative mechanisms.

9. **[JUDGEMENT] The proposed seven-construct combinatorial screen cannot resolve effects ≤10 percentage points at the planned sample sizes (n=66–90).** Statistical power for a ~5–10 point effect at that N is 25–50%. With seven independent tests uncorrected for multiple comparisons, the false-positive rate is ~31%. Screening this broad for effects this small requires either larger N or a prior specification of the target effect size.

## 5. VERDICT AFTER REBUTTAL

**Unchanged in substance, strengthened in justification:**

Do not commit to the K174R-centred p33 + p61/p65 design now. The residue target is refuted, the p61/p65 rationale is unverified, and the timeline is incompatible with reading the source papers.

**Proceed instead with a narrowed three-week plan:**
- Retrieve and read Harper 2016 and Shilts 2026 in full
- Design a reciprocal swap (T36 p33 → T68-1, n ≥ 200)
- Measure source-plant titer in all p33 chimeras
- Drop K174R from any construct
- Treat p61/p65 as exploratory, not lead targets

**What moved between Round 1 and now:** A's distinction between "permissive effects justify exploratory design" and "permissive effects do not establish a containment target" gave me precision I lacked. B's shared-K174 argument closed the residue case completely. D and E correctly elevated titer as the most dangerous uncontrolled confound. The five rebuttals reveal no disagreement on the checkable facts; verdicts diverge only on the decision-theoretic frame ("must all constraints be resolved before proceeding?"). That is not a scientific disagreement — it is a risk tolerance question for the team.

**Confidence in surviving findings: very high.** All six reviewers independently identified the same nine findings. K174R is indefensible. FS577 is unanchored. The 5.2-point gap is noise. The reciprocal is missing. Harper and Shilts remain unread. These are checkable facts, not judgment calls.
