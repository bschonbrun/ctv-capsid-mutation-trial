B. FINDINGS (Checkable facts, in ranking order by consensus strength)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**UNANIMOUS (6/6 reviewers):**

1. [CHECKABLE: residue identity data in brief B5 and Shilts 2020]
   **K174R as a construct target is mathematically impossible.**
   - T36 infectious clone EU937521 encodes K174, not R174
   - T68-1 p33 also encodes K174
   - p33 swap moves T68-1 p33 (K174) into T36 clone (K174)
   - The observed gain (1.5% → 17.8%, 16.3 points) cannot be driven by a
     K↔R change at position 174 because neither sequence changed
   - FS577 and T30 both encode R174 (not K174), yet differ ~15-fold in
     transmission (24.1% vs 1.57%), further falsifying R174 as necessary
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Drop K174R from ANY construct design.

2. [CHECKABLE: sample sizes and named studies]
   **The 5.2-point "residual" attributed to p61/p65 is not a verified gap.**
   - Harper 2016's 23% is unread, lacks a denominator, CI unknown
   - Shilts 2020's 17.8% has CI 10.5–27.3% (n=90)
   - Subtraction: 23% − 17.8% = 5.2 points
   - Cross-study variance, overlapping CIs, unknown assay equivalence
   - Power to detect 5.2 points at n=90/arm: 14%
   - For 80% power: need ~934 per arm
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Do not treat p61/p65 as primary targets. Downgrade to
     exploratory screening. No statistical basis for their inclusion.

3. [CHECKABLE: denominator missing in cited reference]
   **FS577's 24.1% transmission rate has no denominator (N unknown).**
   - Used to establish "16-fold gap" between CPm-high and CPm-low
   - Used to claim CPm sequence variation matters
   - Cannot be statistically evaluated without N
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: FS577 cannot anchor quantitative claims. CPm falsification
     rests on the functional test (16/90 vs 17/90, p=1.00) not the FS577 ratio.

4. [CHECKABLE: fold-change arithmetic]
   **The brief conflates two different fold-changes.**
   - States "16-fold difference between high (T68-1) and low (T36)"
   - T68-1/T36 field = 95/215 ÷ 1/66 = 29.2-fold
   - 16-fold = 24.1% ÷ 1.5% (FS577 vs T36, but FS577 lacks N)
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Use 29.2-fold when referring to T68-1 vs T36. Avoid mixing
     in FS577 without its denominator.

5. [JUDGEMENT: biological mechanism unidentified]
   **The p33 effect is statistically real but mechanistically unidentified.**
   - p33 is a viroporin (documented: affects membrane stability, virion yield)
   - Swap could increase transmission via:
     a) Virion accumulation in source plant (→ higher titer)
     b) Phloem loading efficiency
     c) Virion-aphid binding (the claimed target)
   - No titer measurements in the assays
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Unmeasured source-plant titer is the most dangerous
     uncontrolled confound. Measure it in any future p33 chimeras.

6. [CHECKABLE: experimental design gap]
   **No reciprocal swap (T36 p33 → T68-1 background) has been performed.**
   - All current work: move T68-1 sequence into T36
   - Missing: move T36 p33 into T68-1
   - Cannot distinguish "p33 is causal" from "p33 is permissive in T36"
   - Reciprocal is the single most efficient falsification test
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Design and run reciprocal swap (n ≥ 200) before committing
     to multi-construct screening.

7. [CHECKABLE: source-availability status]
   **Harper 2016 and Shilts 2026 remain unread.**
   - Brief cites Harper as a paywalled journal (methods, denominators unknown)
   - Shilts 2026 cited from abstract only (no methods, no construct details)
   - "Coordinated function of p33, p61, p65" is an abstract claim, unverified
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Retrieve full texts before designing constructs around
     their findings. The 5.2-point gap and p61/p65 inclusion rest on these.

8. [CHECKABLE: arithmetic error in brief]
   **Percentage-point language in brief misstates the numbers.**
   - "p33 explains most of the gap" vs. baseline T36 (1.5%)
   - p33 gain: 16.3 points (documented)
   - Total gap to T68-1: 42.7 points
   - p33 accounts for 38.1% of the total gap, not "most" (>50%)
   - However: p33 accounts for 93.6% of the observed chimera-series gain
     (16.3 of 16.3+1.1 points)
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Restate as "p33 accounts for ~38% of the baseline-to-donor
     gap; ~94% of the gain seen in the p33 and p33+CPm+5'UTR comparison series."

9. [JUDGEMENT: statistical power for multi-construct screen]
   **The proposed 7-construct combinatorial panel has insufficient power.**
   - Planned N: 66–90 per construct
   - Planned comparisons: 7 or more (uncorrected for multiplicity)
   - Power to detect 10-point effects at n=90: 35–47%
   - P(≥1 false positive in 7 independent tests, uncorrected): 30.2%
   - For 80% power on 10-point effects: need ~200–280 per arm
   ⇒ IMPLICATION: Either increase N to ~250/construct, or reduce to 3–4
     prioritized targets with pre-specified effect size. Implement
     multiplicity correction (e.g., Bonferroni α=0.007 for 7 tests).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
**NEAR-UNANIMOUS (5/6 or 5/6 accounting for one explicit recantation):**

10. **CPm sequence variation is falsified, but CPm involvement is not.**
    - 240/240 amino-acid identity between FS577 and T36 field
    - Functional test: 16/90 vs 17/90, Fisher p=1.00 (no detectable CPm effect)
    - These exclude CPm coding-sequence variation; do NOT exclude
      - CPm expression level or timing
      - CPm RNA-structure effects (synonymous codon variation)
      - CPm interactions with p33 or other proteins (epistasis)
    ⇒ Lead with the functional null (p=1.00), not the sequence identity alone.

11. **Experimental units and clustering are undocumented.**
    - Counts: 95/215, 16/90, 1/66
    - Undocumented: whether these are independent inoculation events or
      pooled plant/run/experiment observations
    - If clustered, binomial CIs and Fisher tests are overprecise
    ⇒ IMPLICATION: Document experimental unit structure before invoking
      statistical significance. May need hierarchical/marginal analysis.

12. **The brief's verdict conflates "gain of function" with "transmission
    determinant."**
    - The p33 swap increases transmission (17.8% vs 1.5%, p=0.001)
    - This does NOT establish that p33 DISABLES transmission when removed
    - A gain-of-function mutation is not a containment target
    - Containment target requires: X present → low transmission;
      X absent → high transmission (or vice versa for a disable)
    ⇒ IMPLICATION: Distinguish the phenotype of interest (gain-of-function
      suppression of T36, or loss-of-function in T68-1?) from the data shown.
