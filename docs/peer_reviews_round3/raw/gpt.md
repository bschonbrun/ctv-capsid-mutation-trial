## What this packet got wrong overall

1. **The context contains a material biological error.** CTV is not transmitted between trees only by the brown citrus aphid. Other aphid species, including *Aphis gossypii*, can transmit it, and infected propagative material is another dissemination route. The description of CTV as categorically “benign” is likewise inappropriate for a virus with strain- and host-dependent disease outcomes.
